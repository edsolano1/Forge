Faults and housekeeping

These are repairs rather than taste. B4 and B5 change nothing you can see.

B1

The delete icon's red touch

In the builder, the bin was meant to go red when you touch it and to be drawn a little larger. A later rule overrides both, so it has been small and grey.

Now, touched
Proposed
B2

The pinned background glow

An old rule with too much weight pins the ambient glow to the top left corner. Forge carries its own fix, so you have never seen it. Worth repairing before the other themes come back.

B3

The level-up leak

The level-up sheet's row styling was written without naming its own sheet, so it also lands on another sheet's rows. Harmless today because that sheet is unreachable.

B4

Delete the old board design

The timeline-with-dots board that came before the day column is still in the file, fully written and fully overridden. It is what keeps the old colours alive.

B5

Sweep the dead styles

Fifty-two classes styled but never drawn, a sheet nobody can open, a hidden share box with no way in, and about two dozen functions nothing calls.

How to answer

Name the codes. "Do T1 to T4 and S1, S2" is enough. If you want one built first so you can see it on your phone before the rest, say which.
