Instructions and the builder, three ways

The two screens that still wear the old glass: the instructions sheet you open from a lift, and each exercise card in the workout builder. Each direction draws both. Dumbbell or Kettlebell becomes a proper control in all three.

Today v5.167

For comparison. Warm glass, thin borders, the choice as two flat chips.

A. Plates the board's card, opened up

The sheet and each builder card become blackened plates with the ember rail, exactly the cards on your week. Dumbbell or Kettlebell is a two-way switch like Week / Month / All. Cues are a numbered ledger. In the builder the sets are the session ledger's cells, and the superset link wears violet.

B. Ledger the session screen's table

The sheet is a dark header band with the name and the pictures, then a ledger of labelled rows: Setup, Cue, Cue, Cue, Mistake. Dumbbell or Kettlebell are two cells, the chosen one lit gold like a cell mid-edit. The builder card is the session ledger with a tools row: measure, one for all or set each one, and the sets as weight and reps cells.

C. Seal the finish screen's plate

Warmer and rounder: the finish screen's stamped plate. The sheet carries a small seal in its corner, the choice is two pills with the chosen one in ember, cues are marked with lit points instead of numbers. The builder card is the same warm plate with each set as one rounded row.

Either way

  • The figures, the words and every builder control keep doing what they do. Only the look changes.
  • The measure picker (Weight and reps, Reps, Seconds) and One for all / Set each one stay; they move into the card's tools row or line.
  • Mix if you like: the sheet from one, the card from another.
