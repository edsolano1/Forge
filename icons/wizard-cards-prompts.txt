FORGE WIZARD CARD ART. 33 SQUARE PICTURES.

Read the STYLE block first. It applies to every picture. Then make the groups one at a time, all pictures in a group in one go, so they match each other exactly. Return each picture as its own 1024 x 1024 PNG named as shown.

STYLE (applies to all 33)
- Flat, single colour on a dark ground. Ember orange #D97B2E on near black #0A0605.
- One pale gold #E8B86D highlight is allowed per picture, at most one small element.
- Either a solid silhouette or a single weight line drawing. Pick one for the whole set and keep it.
- No gradients, no shadows, no texture, no photo realism, no 3D. Think enamel pin or woodcut.
- Square. Subject centred, filling about 60 percent of the frame, even empty margin all round.
- No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere in the picture.
- Same line weight and same level of detail in every picture. They sit side by side in an app.
- The app's mark is a hammer coming down on an anvil. Forge, ember, iron and heat are on brand.
- Nothing cartoonish. No faces, no people, no mascots.
- Must be easy to trace into a clean vector afterwards: few shapes, clean edges, no tiny detail.
