Level up, the way games do it

What every good level-up screen shares: it takes over the screen for a moment, one bright thing sits in the middle, the numbers count up rather than appear, each gain lands in sequence with a sound, and the palette jumps to white-hot and gold with one contrasting colour. Three ways to bring that to Forge. Motion is described; these are stills.

S. The banner

A beam of light cuts across the dark with LEVEL UP stamped on it, the way a banner slides in when a rank goes up. Below it the lifts land one at a time, each number counting up from the old to the new with a tick, the gain in bright green beside it. Cleanest read, biggest moment.

T. The burst

The anvil is struck and light bursts from it, sparks flying. Each lift is a hot gold plate with the number counting up large; an approximate jump gets the app's violet instead of gold so the colour says it without a word. Most colour, most forge.

U. The stat sheet

The box you see when a creature levels up: a framed panel, each stat on its own row, and the rows tick up one after the other with a click, the current one lit. The gains stack in green down the right. Calm, legible, and very satisfying in motion.
