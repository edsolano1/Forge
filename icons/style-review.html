Style review: the whole app

I walked every screen and sheet at your phone's width, and ran three code audits over the stylesheet. This page is what I found, worst first. Nothing here is built. Tell me which parts to do and I build those.

1. The mono font never loadsone line fixes it

The biggest one. The Forge theme names four typefaces: Big Shoulders for signage, Barlow for body, Marcellus for names, and Share Tech Mono for numbers. But the theme never defines the mono variable, so 114 rules that ask for mono silently fall back to Barlow. Every uppercase label, every count, every chip you have seen this week is in the wrong face and has been for a long time.

4. The small stuffinvisible one by one

  • Four radii for one shape. Pill buttons are 999px in seven places, 20px in three, 19px in one, 18px in three. One value.
  • Three trackings on the same uppercase label, .12em, .14em and .16em, sometimes on neighbouring buttons in one sheet.
  • Two selected washes, gold at 6 percent and at 8 percent, for the identical chosen state.
  • Near-miss colours. The stylesheet holds 194 distinct hexes, 131 used exactly once. A dozen sit one digit from a palette colour, for example a border at #4a2718 beside the real #4a2c1c. These are the fingerprints of the old era.
  • Set cells use a 7px radius and a darker border than the anvil button beside them on the same row.
  • Blur. Two backdrop blurs survive, on sheet backgrounds and the session header. Everything else is opaque now.

5. Textureyou asked

Plates are flat fills today. Four ways to give them a surface, each cheap and static. My pick is the first, at a whisper, on sheets and big plates only.

6. Rules fighting each othertwo you can see

The stylesheet declares some classes twice, far apart, and the later one silently wins. Two of these are live faults.

  • The delete icon never turns red. In the builder, the bin was given a red warning colour on touch, and a later rule overrides it with plain grey. It has been silent since.
  • That same icon is drawn smaller than intended, 13px where its own rule asks for 15px.
  • The ambient glow behind the app is pinned to the top left corner by an old rule with too much weight. Forge has its own fix, so you have never seen it; the other themes would.
  • The level-up sheet's row styling leaks onto another sheet, because it was written without naming its own sheet.
  • A whole superseded board design is still in the file, the timeline with dots that came before the day column. It is dead, and it is what keeps the old colours alive in the stylesheet.

7. Dead weighthousekeeping

  • A whole sheet nobody can open, the old "Which day does it go on?".
  • The share-as-text box on the finish screen, left hidden with no way in, from before Share moved.
  • Three superseded board and card rule blocks that keep the old colours alive in the file.
  • About two dozen functions whose only mention is their own definition.
  • Six classes drawn by the app with no styling at all, including the warm-up toggle and the Pump day card.
  • Fifty-two classes styled but never drawn, including a fully dressed button with eleven rules that nothing emits, and an entire workout wrap-up panel from before the finish screen.

What I would do, in order

  • One. Turn the mono on, and set the name role to Marcellus. That is most of the inconsistency in two lines.
  • Two. The four missed surfaces: week footer buttons, rest timer, tour card, wizard cards.
  • Three. The small stuff as one sweep: radii, tracking, washes, near-miss colours.
  • Four. Texture, if you like one of the four.
  • Five. Dead weight, which changes nothing you can see.
