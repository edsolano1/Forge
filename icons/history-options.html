Workouts finished, three directions

The same fortnight in each: two sessions this week, one of them with a record, and the day views underneath. All three keep Week, Month and All, keep "Add a walk", and move Copy all and Save as a file behind a Share button so the list gets the room.

AD. The ledger week

The board's language, looking backward. The week is a row of day columns like the home screen, a gold stop on trained days, a brighter one where a record fell, a hollow one on a skipped day. Three iron tiles for done, sets and records. Each session is a plate with its gold and violet counts.

AE. The seal roll

Every finished session is the seal from the finish screen, rolled up small: the workout, the date, the sets as the hero, a record chip when one fell. The week is a line of seven stamps, the trained days struck and turned. One line above says the week in words.
