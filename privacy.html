Forge — Privacy

Last updated 29 August 2026

Forge does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data anywhere. There are no accounts, no servers holding your information, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Everything you log stays on your device.

What Forge stores, and where

Your workouts, logged sets, personal records, walks, notes and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. Nobody — including the developer — can see them.

Because the data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app deletes it. Forge includes a Backup & restore feature so you can save a copy of your own data to a file and keep it wherever you choose.

Health Connect

If you allow it, Forge reads walking and exercise data from Health Connect so that walks recorded by your watch or another fitness app appear in your training history without being typed in twice. Specifically, Forge requests permission to read:

  • Exercise sessions
  • Steps
  • Distance
  • Total calories burned
  • Heart rate
  • The above in the background, so a walk syncs without the app being open

Forge only reads. It never writes to Health Connect, and it never sends Health Connect data off your device. What it reads is used to show a walk in your own history and nothing else. You can withdraw these permissions at any time in Health Connect, and Forge continues to work without them.

The watch app

The Forge watch app talks only to the Forge app on your own paired phone, over the connection Wear OS provides between them. It sends the set you logged and receives what to show next. Nothing goes anywhere else.

Network

Forge loads its own interface from the developer's website when you open it, so it can stay up to date, and works offline afterwards. Like any website request, this involves your device contacting that server; no information about your workouts is included in that request, and nothing about you is recorded there beyond the ordinary server logs any website receives.

Children

Forge is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
